FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly does Bolblar Agent automate?

The whole pipeline: scripting, storyboarding, generating footage, voiceover, music, captions, editing, brand checks, exporting in every aspect ratio and publishing. You can add approval steps anywhere you want a human in the loop.

Do I own the videos it creates?

Yes. Everything you generate is yours to use commercially, on any channel.

Can it use my own footage, logos and voice?

Yes. Upload a brand kit and existing clips and the agent will mix them with generated shots. On Pro and above you can also create a custom brand voice.

Which platforms can it publish to?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn and X out of the box, plus delivery to Google Drive, Slack or any destination via webhook.

What happens if I don’t like a result?

Tell the agent what to change in plain English — “faster pacing”, “swap the second scene”, “warmer voice” — and it re-renders just the parts that need it.
